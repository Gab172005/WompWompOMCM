# Data sources and attribution

OMCM distinguishes public source evidence from synthetic modeling inputs. No real
patient records are included. The [dataset guide](README.md) documents imports;
[methodology](../docs/methodology.md) explains their interpretation.

| Asset | Source and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Community inputs | Supplied `laguna_barangays.csv`: PSA-style demographic records, municipal income class, and synthetic morbidity for 681 barangays |
| Hazard inputs | Supplied `laguna_barangay_risk_index.csv`: point-screening evidence, with source codes and missing values retained; not an area-wide flood-depth map |
| Hospital register | Supplied `laguna_hospitals.csv`: DOH/PhilHealth verification labels, coordinates, bed-count dates, and source notes retained per record |
| Boundaries | 2023 PSGC snapshot from [philippines-json-maps](https://github.com/faeldon/philippines-json-maps), derived from [philippines-psgc-shapefiles](https://github.com/altcoder/philippines-psgc-shapefiles) |
| Fixed clinics | [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) clinic observations, acquired through Overpass and attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL 1.0 |
| Road pilot | Bundled Los Baños–Bay routing fixture and its embedded source metadata; not a complete Laguna road matrix |
| Model history | Reproducible fictional illness/weather sequences from `backend/services/forecast_history.py` |
| Appointment training | Synthetic observations generated by `backend/scripts/train_demand_model.py` |

## Reproducible provenance

Boundary provenance, a pinned upstream commit, feature counts, and checksums are
in [source.json](../frontend/public/assets/geo/source.json). The upstream MIT
license is retained in [LICENSE.txt](../frontend/public/assets/geo/LICENSE.txt).
This is a historical snapshot, not current official certification.

Clinic [acquisition metadata](../frontend/public/assets/osm/osm-clinics-provenance.json)
records the actual query, endpoint, retrieval time, OSM database timestamp,
checksums, joins, and warnings. The
[raw response](../frontend/public/assets/osm/osm-clinics-raw.json) and
[normalized records](../frontend/public/assets/osm/osm-clinics-normalized.json)
are kept for reproducibility and attribution. The bundled acquisition was
retrieved on 2026-10-23; it is not a live clinic registry.

Model artifacts and [illness forecast metadata](../backend/data/models/illness-forecast-v2.metadata.json)
and [appointment demand metadata](../backend/data/models/appointment-demand-v1.metadata.json)
record feature definitions, synthetic generators, evaluation splits, errors,
limitations, and model checksums. Their errors do not establish clinical accuracy.

## Use and limitations

Source verification labels do not prove current facility operation or bed
availability. Missing observations are not verified absence. Straight-line
facility distances can miss barriers and outside-province access. Hazard point
screening does not establish parcel safety or precise inundation boundaries.
Retain upstream attribution and source metadata when redistributing or updating
assets; review source terms before operational use.
